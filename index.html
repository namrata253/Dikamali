Dikamali

Botanical Name: Gardenia gummifera (L.f.)

Common Name: Dikamali / Indian Gardenia

Family: Rubiaceae

Description: Dikamali is a small shrub with green leaves and fragrant flowers. It produces a yellowish resin called Dikamali.

Medicinal Uses

  1. Traditionally used in Ayurvedic preparations, especially for digestive and intestinal problems.
  2. Traditionally used to help with stomach discomfort.
  3. Traditionally used for inflammation-related complaints.
